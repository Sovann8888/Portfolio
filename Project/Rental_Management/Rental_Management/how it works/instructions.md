

Landlord side

1. Welcome: Log in with your phone number and choose "I am a Landlord."  
2. Dashboard shows Property Management overview — total rooms managed, and a Room Directory listing each room's floor, renter, rent, and status (vacant/occupied).  
3. Tapping a room opens its profile — renter info, rent fee, payment status, next due date, and any maintenance issues.  
4. Meter Calculator — enter previous & new water/electricity meter readings plus their rates, and it automatically calculates the utility cost breakdown.  
5. Invoice Creator — auto-generates a digital invoice (room rent \+ water \+ electricity \= total due) and sends it to the renter with a KHQR payment code.  
6. Payment Checker — record new payments (ABA Bank/PayWay, amount, room, date) into a payment ledger, and share an ABA QR code so renters can pay directly.  
7. Maintenance Requests — tracks repair requests from renters, with counts for Open / In Progress / Resolved this month.

Renter (tenant) side

1. Welcome dashboard — shows room info, current bill (rent \+ utilities, due date), and latest water/electric meter readings, plus notifications (e.g. "meter reading due," "landlord viewed your report").  
2. Submit Meter Reading — just take a live photo of the water and electricity meters; AI reads the numbers automatically instead of manual entry.  
3. Request Maintenance — submit a new issue (type, description, urgency level) and track status under "My Requests."  
4. My History — one place to see all past payments and submitted meter readings.
