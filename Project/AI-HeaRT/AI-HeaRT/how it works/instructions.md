1. Welcome: Log in using your phone number and verify via the OTP notification — one number per patient.  
2. Patients can input their symptoms.  
3. They can also search for their symptoms using the search bar.  
4. It analyzes all your input symptoms then calculates the probability of matching conditions.  
5. Shows the most likely condition with its match %, risk level (e.g. High Risk / Emergency), and matched symptoms.  
6. Also lists other possible conditions, ranked by probability, along with their risk levels.  
7. Tapping a condition opens its Overview — a plain-language explanation of what it is and how serious it is.  
8. Shows the Symptoms tab — which of your symptoms matched, and which remaining symptoms of that disease you don't have.  
9. Shows the Precautions tab — steps to take (and why) for that condition.  
10. Shows the Diet tab — recommended foods and why they help.  
11. Shows the Workout tab — safe activity recommendations for that condition.  
12. Shows the Medication tab — informational medication guidance (not a prescription — always consult a doctor/pharmacist).  
13. For low-risk results, it gives a "Home Care" verdict instead of an emergency warning.  
14. Opens the Home Care Plan — a personalized day-by-day plan (Care Steps, Diet, Activity, Medication, Daily Tracker) based on the patient's profile.
