Attendance System

Automated QR-based classroom check-ins with network-restricted, refreshing tokens.

github.com/Sovann8888/attendance-system

// HOW IT WORKS

01Professor creates an account with full name + email, then verifies with the code sent — one account per professor.
02Professor adds students to a class — student ID, full name, major (optional), and subject.
03Professor views all their classes on one dashboard (e.g. Cyber Security, Data Science & AI), each showing student count and session count.
04Opening a class shows students enrolled, sessions held, and a button to Start QR Attendance Session.
05Starting a session generates a live QR code ("Scan to Mark Attendance") that refreshes every second, restricted to students on school WiFi, with a live counter of students marked.
06Once a session ends, it's logged in the Attendance Session History (date/time, professor, status), and each student's record updates (e.g. sessions attended 0/1, 0/2) — exportable as CSV.

Smart-RentHouse (LordAI)

Landlord & tenant portals for billing, meter OCR and payment verification.

github.com/Sovann8888/community-rental-management

// HOW IT WORKS — LANDLORD SIDE

01Log in with your phone number and choose "I am a Landlord."
02Dashboard shows a Property Management overview — total rooms managed, and a Room Directory listing each room's floor, renter, rent, and status (vacant/occupied).
03Tapping a room opens its profile — renter info, rent fee, payment status, next due date, and any maintenance issues.
04Meter Calculator — enter previous & new water/electricity meter readings plus their rates, and it automatically calculates the utility cost breakdown.
05Invoice Creator — auto-generates a digital invoice (room rent + water + electricity = total due) and sends it to the renter with a KHQR payment code.
06Payment Checker — record new payments (ABA Bank/PayWay, amount, room, date) into a payment ledger, and share an ABA QR code so renters can pay directly.
07Maintenance Requests — tracks repair requests from renters, with counts for Open / In Progress / Resolved this month.

// HOW IT WORKS — RENTER SIDE

01Welcome dashboard — shows room info, current bill (rent + utilities, due date), and latest water/electric meter readings, plus notifications (e.g. "meter reading due," "landlord viewed your report").
02Submit Meter Reading — just take a live photo of the water and electricity meters; AI reads the numbers automatically instead of manual entry.
03Request Maintenance — submit a new issue (type, description, urgency level) and track status under "My Requests."
04My History — one place to see all past payments and submitted meter readings.

AI-HeaRT

AI probability-matching health triage engine with OTP auth and personalized home-care guidance.

github.com/Sovann8888/AI-HeaRT

// HOW IT WORKS

01Welcome: Log in using your phone number and verify via the OTP notification — one number per patient.
02Patients can input their symptoms.
03They can also search for their symptoms using the search bar.
04It analyzes all input symptoms then calculates the probability of matching conditions.
05Shows the most likely condition with its match %, risk level (e.g. High Risk / Emergency), and matched symptoms.
06Also lists other possible conditions, ranked by probability, along with their risk levels.
07Tapping a condition opens its Overview — a plain-language explanation of what it is and how serious it is.
08Shows the Symptoms tab — which of your symptoms matched, and which remaining symptoms of that disease you don't have.
09Shows the Precautions tab — steps to take (and why) for that condition.
10Shows the Diet tab — recommended foods and why they help.
11Shows the Workout tab — safe activity recommendations for that condition.
12Shows the Medication tab — informational medication guidance (not a prescription — always consult a doctor/pharmacist).
13For low-risk results, it gives a "Home Care" verdict instead of an emergency warning.
14Opens the Home Care Plan — a personalized day-by-day plan (Care Steps, Diet, Activity, Medication, Daily Tracker) based on the patient's profile.
